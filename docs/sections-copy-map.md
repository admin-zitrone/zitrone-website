## Strategic Alignment

All copy updates align with:

1. **Market Point of View:** Food service as an operational layer, not a vendor service list
2. **Brand Pillars:** Designed around workday, thoughtful food, human care, operational trust
3. **Value Hierarchy:** Flows from operational reliability → less friction → better workday → stronger teams
4. **Differentiation:** Zitrone owns the infrastructure; no vendor gaps; transparent operations
